# GENESIS — Game Design Foundation v0.1

**Status:** Pre-production foundation  
**Decision labels:** Confirmed / Proposed / Open / Risk

## 1. High concept

A 2D side-scrolling Action Metroidvania about the journey of a human life inside the mother's womb, from a single cell to birth.

The player explores a fantasy-biological ecosystem, absorbs matter, evolves their body, survives threats and gradually discovers the emotional meaning of their own beginning.

**Working title:** GENESIS — The Journey Before Life  
**Tagline:** "Every life begins with a journey." (Provisional; not approved.)

## 2. Player experience

The intended emotional identity is **Become Human**: the player begins as something simple and vulnerable, then gradually becomes more capable and recognizably human.

The emotional message centers on parents and the love, effort and sacrifice that make life possible.

Tone: balanced between wonder, hardship and emotion.

Audience: broad.

## 3. Genre & references

- 2D side-scrolling Action Metroidvania.
- Inspirations include Ori, Hollow Knight and Rockman / Mega Man.
- The game must establish its own visual and mechanical identity rather than imitate any one reference.
- PC / Steam first; consoles may follow.

## 4. Pillars

### Evolution
The body is progression. Biological materials and evolution choices change the player's capabilities and appearance.

### Combat
Combat begins with vulnerability and opportunistic survival, then develops into body-part weapons, biological powers and build-specific combos.

### Science
The world is inspired by real biology but reinterpreted as a fantastical ecosystem. It is not a strict medical or biological simulation.

### Emotion
The setting and parents' presence are revealed gradually through environmental storytelling, memories, sound and visual details.

## 5. Player character

- No name and no dialogue; the player projects themselves into the journey.
- Begins as a simple cell / amorphous organic form.
- Body evolves gradually while retaining a recognizable silhouette at major points.
- Different builds may create visibly different forms.
- Major evolution milestones should have memorable visual and audio feedback.

## 6. World

- Entirely imagined fantasy-biological world.
- Multiple interconnected major regions.
- Friendly, neutral and hostile organisms.
- Exploration rewards resources, secrets, science/lore, parental context and environmental mysteries.
- The mother is a nurturing and protective presence, not an enemy or dungeon boss.

Potential region names discussed as brainstorming only; none are locked.

## 7. Movement & combat progression

### Early game
- Swimming.
- Crawling on surfaces.
- Gripping terrain.
- Body contraction.
- Hiding and survival.
- Opportunistic biting, collision or absorption rather than full combat.

### Later game
- Run, jump, double-jump, dash.
- Agile evasion and potentially gliding.
- Body-part weapons and biological powers.
- Multiple-button combos and mastery-oriented combat.
- Basic movement is free; special abilities may consume energy.

### Difficulty
Accessible exploration with more demanding boss encounters that reward learning patterns and using evolved abilities.

## 8. Core loop

**Explore → Absorb → Evolve → Adapt → Overcome → Explore further**

New body parts should provide at least one meaningful function:
- Combat.
- Traversal.
- Environmental interaction.

## 9. Resources & economy

Potential resources:
- Nutrition.
- Calcium / minerals.
- Energy.
- Protein / organic matter.
- Genetic material.

Resource sources:
- Defeating organisms.
- Secret areas.
- Absorbing environmental material and organisms.

Resources may support evolution, body upgrades, special abilities and environmental interactions.

**Open:** Whether these become separate resource meters, crafting materials or a smaller abstract resource system.

HUD direction: minimal; detailed information belongs in the evolution interface. Resources may be represented through the body and environment.

On death, some resources are dropped and can be retrieved. Environmental resources may regenerate.

## 10. Evolution & builds

Current direction:
- 4–5 major evolution paths (proposed).
- Multiple branches may develop in parallel, limited by resources.
- Some body parts upgrade; others unlock new capabilities.
- Evolution choices are permanent.
- Acquired parts remain owned; equipped capacity is limited and may expand.
- Build affects combat, movement and environmental interaction.
- New Game+, multiple endings and secrets are replayability ideas, not MVP commitments.

**Critical risk:** Permanent choices must never create an unrecoverable build or block mandatory progression.

## 11. World structure & progression

- One interconnected world divided into major regions.
- Main objectives should be clear, with optional branches and secrets.
- New abilities open routes; skilled players may sequence-break.
- Some areas may signal that the player should return later.
- Checkpoints are biological shelters that save, heal and allow development.
- Major bosses test learned skills and may have multiple solutions based on build.

A 7–9 stage developmental structure was selected as a direction, but it is a scope risk and may be consolidated.

## 12. Narrative & ending

Reveal the womb setting gradually.

Storytelling devices:
- Memories and visual fragments.
- Environmental objects and traces.
- Mother's heartbeat, voice, movement and protection, used selectively.
- Rare details and memories connecting the father to the story.
- Occasional outside-world perspectives.

Ending:
- A playable final stage.
- Birth as the culmination, followed by a cinematic.
- Intended emotion: happiness with a little sadness as the player leaves the womb.

No post-birth life simulation is currently planned.

## 13. Art & audio direction

### Art
- 2D dark fantasy influence.
- Anatomical inspiration with strong fantasy reinterpretation.
- Environments should be beautiful, vibrant, mysterious and sometimes dangerous.
- Character is abstract, organic, strange but beautiful.
- Evolution should change the character during play and through dedicated milestone animations.
- Avoid visual imitation of Hollow Knight; develop a distinct life-centric visual language.

### Audio
- Orchestral cinematic and dark fantasy direction.
- Biological and fantastical environmental sounds.
- Distinct boss themes, potentially changing by phase.
- Evolution sound should feel biological, magical and triumphant.
- Heartbeat should be used sparingly for emotional impact.

## 14. Scope & production

- Solo + AI; no fixed deadline.
- Initial target: 4–6 hours.
- Expansion to 8–12 hours only if the core loop and production capacity justify it.
- Godot is preferred for the first prototype; Unity remains an alternative until validated.
- Frame-by-frame animation is the current direction, but production cost must be tested.
- Build a vertical slice before full production.
- AI may assist with concepts, code, audio exploration, testing and production assets, with consistent human review.

## 15. Release direction

- PC / Steam first.
- English first; additional languages later.
- Current preference is to skip a public demo and focus on a complete release; revisit this decision later.
- Price is undecided.
- The project is both a passion project and a possible long-term indie brand. Commercial commitment remains open.

## 16. Non-goals for the first prototype

- Full 7–9 stage world.
- All 4–5 evolution branches.
- Full five-resource economy.
- Complete narrative cinematics.
- New Game+ and multiple endings.
- Console support.
- Final Steam pricing and marketing assets.

The prototype exists to validate movement, absorption, survival and one meaningful evolution.
