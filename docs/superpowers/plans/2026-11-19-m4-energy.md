# M4 Energy for the Spike Burst Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Add a small energy pool to the evolved spike burst and verify it in the prototype.

**Architecture:** Keep energy state and attack affordability on `PlayerController`; update the existing prototype HUD to show it after evolution. Reuse the predator and scene, add a focused headless regression script, and leave energy out of the save file.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, existing headless script checks and PowerShell verification runner.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-m4-energy-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Use Godot 4.7.2 stable.
- Proposed starting values: maximum 3 energy, 1 energy per burst, regeneration 1 energy per second.
- Energy only limits the optional burst; it never gates movement, absorption, evolution or required progression.
- Do not persist energy; preserve the existing evolved and nutrition save behavior.
- Keep the change small and reversible; do not extract shared systems for one prototype scene.

## Review Focus

- Failed or cooldown-blocked bursts must not spend energy — test no-cost rejection.
- Energy must never exceed its maximum — test capped regeneration.
- Energy cannot be spent before evolution — test pre-evolution denial.
- Retry must restore full energy without changing saved evolution/nutrition — test revive reset and run existing save flow.
- HUD should not imply energy is available before evolution — inspect the evolved and unevolved HUD states in the existing scene flow.
