# Muse Brief — Protagonist & Character Evolution

**Status:** Exploration brief; evolution stages and designs remain proposed.

## Objective

Explore how one protagonist can evolve from a single cell into a complex pre-birth form while remaining recognizable as the same player character.

The emotional idea is **Become Human**: the player begins vulnerable and simple, then gradually becomes capable and recognizably human.

## Character constraints

- The protagonist has no name and no dialogue.
- The earliest form should be simple, readable and expressive through movement.
- The character should feel organic and alive, not like a conventional armored hero shrunk down.
- Major transformations should preserve one or more identity cues (silhouette rhythm, core nucleus/center, color accent, movement signature or another proposed motif).
- Different builds may look different, but should still read as the same protagonist.
- Do not make the mother or womb itself an enemy.

## Explore

1. Single-cell forms: several silhouettes and internal structures.
2. Early organism: how locomotion, sensing and absorption can be suggested visually.
3. Transitional forms: gradual emergence of differentiated body parts.
4. More capable forms: possible limbs, protective structures and biological weapons.
5. Late pre-birth form: a coherent, elegant culmination, not necessarily a miniature adult human.

Create:
- A silhouette sheet with strong black/white readability.
- A side-by-side evolution lineup (roughly 5–7 illustrative stages for exploration only).
- 2–3 alternate evolution paths showing how builds might change the silhouette.
- A close-up study of distinctive materials, surface details and color accents.
- Optional rough transformation keyframes for one milestone.

## Gameplay hypotheses to annotate

For each body feature, suggest a possible function:
- Movement / swimming / crawling / gripping.
- Defense / evasion.
- Absorption / sensing.
- Combat / special ability.
- Environmental interaction.

Do not add body parts only for decoration unless they reinforce identity or emotion.

## Deliverable

A small character concept pack with labeled alternatives, evolution continuity notes and open questions. Do not create a full production sprite sheet yet.
