# Muse Brief — First Playable Region

**Status:** Exploration brief; region identity and layout are not approved.

## Objective

Imagine the first small region the player explores as a vulnerable early organism. It should teach the world through visual storytelling and support the first movement, absorption and survival mechanics.

## Experience goals

- Convey wonder, scale and vulnerability.
- Make the space feel biological without looking like a literal medical diagram.
- Offer readable surfaces and routes for swimming, crawling and gripping.
- Include safe pockets, resource opportunities and a mild threat.
- Give the player a reason to explore a little beyond the obvious path.

## Explore

Create 2–3 alternative environment concepts:
- A wide establishing view of the region.
- A closer gameplay-scale section showing foreground, traversable surfaces and background layers.
- One safe biological shelter/checkpoint idea.
- One resource-rich pocket and one dangerous pocket.
- A few visual landmarks that can help navigation.

Potential motifs to explore: membrane caverns, nutrient streams, branching fibers, translucent walls, mineral deposits, cellular gardens. These are prompts, not fixed content.

## Layout considerations

- This is a 2D side-scrolling game. Show a plausible playable horizontal route.
- Keep foreground gameplay surfaces distinct from decorative background.
- Consider how the camera reveals landmarks and threats.
- Avoid visual clutter that hides the player or hazards.
- The concept should be reducible into reusable tiles, modular props and parallax layers.

## Gameplay hypotheses

Annotate where the player might:
- Swim or drift.
- Crawl or grip.
- Absorb a resource.
- Hide from or avoid a creature.
- Discover an optional secret.
- Reach a safe shelter.

Do not design a full map or commit to exact region progression yet.

## Deliverable

A first-region moodboard plus one annotated gameplay-space concept and a list of environmental gameplay ideas.
