# Muse Brief — GENESIS Visual Identity

**Status:** Exploration brief; nothing here is approved art direction.

## Objective

Find a distinct visual language for GENESIS: an imagined biological world that feels alive, mysterious, beautiful and occasionally dangerous. The result should work as a readable 2D side-scrolling game and be feasible for a solo developer to reproduce.

## Explore

Create 3–4 genuinely different art-direction routes. For each route, show:
- A small palette with primary, secondary and accent colors.
- Material studies: membrane, tissue, fluid, mineral, organic fibers, translucent structures.
- Lighting and atmosphere.
- Shape language (soft, branching, layered, crystalline, flowing, etc.).
- A small environment fragment and one creature to test consistency.
- A short description of the emotional impression.

Potential directions to explore, not requirements:
- Luminous organic wonder with strong color contrast.
- Darker mysterious biological fantasy with selective bioluminescence.
- Soft translucent micro-world with rich layered depth.
- Bold graphic shapes with painterly lighting and organic textures.

## Constraints

- Avoid recognizable imitation of Ori, Hollow Knight or any single reference.
- Do not make the entire world uniformly dark, red, wet or horror-like.
- Keep silhouettes and gameplay objects readable against backgrounds.
- Avoid excessive micro-detail that would disappear at game camera scale.
- Concepts should be adaptable to layered 2D backgrounds and reusable asset families.

## Deliverable

A comparison board of 3–4 directions, plus a recommendation with trade-offs. Do not decide for the project owner.
